Materials

Granite, quartz, marble — and much more.

Each stone behaves a little differently. The short version, so you can pick what fits how you live.

Granite

Natural rock, cut into slabs — no two alike.

  • Hard and strong
  • A unique pattern in every slab
  • Scratch and stain resistant
  • Resists most acids

Quartz

Low maintenance, with a big color selection.

  • Non-absorbent
  • Hygienic surface
  • Stain resistant
  • A large selection of colors

Marble

The elegant veining people stop and look at.

  • Elegant, one-of-a-kind veining
  • More absorbent than granite
  • Needs a little extra care — we’ll tell you what

Looking for something not on this list? Ask — we work with more than these three.

Edge profiles

Pick your edge.

The edge is the shape you see and touch along the front of the counter. These are drawn to scale against each other, so a 2 1/4" mitered edge really is three times as thick as a 3/4" slab. Tap one to see it up close, then add it to your quote.

How it goes

From bare cabinets to finished stone, step by step.

We walk you through each part so you always know what’s next.

  1. 1

    Measure & template

    We come out once the cabinets are in, measure everything and template the layout — sink, cooktop, seams and overhangs.

  2. 2

    Pick your slab

    Choose the material, color and edge. Want to see stone in person? Come by the shop — by appointment.

  3. 3

    Fabricate

    The slab is cut to the template, the sink and cooktop cutouts are made, and your edge profile is shaped and polished.

  4. 4

    Install & seal

    We set the tops, make the seams, mount the sink, seal the stone where it needs it and clean up.

Every job is a little different — we’ll tell you what to expect for yours before we start.
